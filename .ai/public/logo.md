Canonical SpinShare brand logo assets served at `/logo/spinshare-logo.png` in the web app and mirrored at the monorepo root for shared reference.

| Path | Role |
|------|------|

- Source of truth for updates: `web/public/spinshare-logo.png` (user drop-in location).
